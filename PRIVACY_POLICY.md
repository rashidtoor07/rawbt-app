# BT Print — Privacy Policy

_Effective date: [DATE YOU PUBLISH]_

BT Print ("the app") is published by VisionWebLabs. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## Summary

BT Print does **not** collect, upload, sell or share any personal data. Everything the app does happens on your device and between your device and your Bluetooth printer.

## Information the app handles

| Information | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Bluetooth address of the printer you select | To reconnect to your printer | Saved only on your device. Removed when you tap **Stop Service** or uninstall the app. |
| Content you print (receipts, text, barcodes, QR codes, web pages) | To send it to your printer | Sent directly to your Bluetooth printer. It is not stored or sent anywhere else. |
| Names and addresses of nearby Bluetooth devices | To show printers you can choose from | Shown on screen only. Not stored or shared. |

The app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services.

## Permissions

- **Nearby devices / Bluetooth**: to find, connect to and print on your Bluetooth printer.
- **Location** (Android 11 and older only): Android requires this permission to scan for Bluetooth devices on those versions. The app never reads or stores your location.
- **Network access**: used only for a print server that listens on your own device (127.0.0.1), so other apps and web pages on the same phone can send print jobs. It is not reachable from other devices, and the app does not connect to the internet.
- **Foreground service**: keeps the print server running so print jobs from other apps work while BT Print is in the background. A notification is shown while it runs.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
